GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked.

The essentials before your first creation.

What can Pixfun do today?+

Import a video, explore its scenes, save ideas and references, and add timed text to a vertical MP4.

The before-and-after scene is a demo. AI video generation, person replacement, translation, dubbing, transcription, and automatic posting are not available yet.

Can I use a link or upload a file?+

Both. Upload MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, or another supported video file up to 500 MB. You can also import a YouTube or other supported video link.

Links depend on platform support, your connection, and access permissions. If a link does not work, upload a file you have permission to use.

Where are my videos stored?+

Your videos, notes, and references stay on this computer in Pixfun’s local folder. They are not sent to third-party AI services. Importing a video link still needs an internet connection.

Do I need to sign up?+

No account or paid AI service is needed for the current local tools. Start with a video and create on your own computer.

Can I reuse someone else’s video?+

Only use footage you own or have permission to use. Adding text does not change ownership. Pixfun helps you explore creative structure; it does not guarantee views or performance.
