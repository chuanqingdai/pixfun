BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few things to know.

Is the Mac app available now?

Pixfun for Mac is in development and is not open for public download. Apply for early access to share your needs and leave your email. We have not announced a launch date.

How can Pixfun help with a large footage library?

AI models help describe your clips, break longer videos into useful scenes, and suggest moments worth keeping. Search by what happens in the footage instead of relying on filenames, so you can review relevant moments without replaying everything. Processing time depends on your Mac and the amount and length of footage.

Can I analyze and find footage without making a video?

Yes. Ask for clip summaries, useful time ranges, or footage matching a scene or action. Review the results and editing suggestions first; creating a video is a separate step.

What can I explore on the website?

Watch five-second highlights, then open each example to explore the full video, visual descriptions, editable segments, and suggested story roles. These are precomputed, editorially reviewed examples. The workflow preview is a demonstration, not a live video generator.

What happens after I apply?

Your answers and email are saved for application review and product research. We may contact you for feedback or an invitation when early access is ready. Applying does not guarantee access, and no confirmation email is sent automatically.

How much will a subscription cost?

Pricing has not been set. The last application question asks what you would consider paying in USD per month if Pixfun solves your editing needs. You can enter 0 or choose “Not sure yet.” Applying does not subscribe you or charge you anything.

Is Pixfun only for quick highlight reels?

We’re exploring travel vlogs, personal diaries, cinematic films, and longer stories—not just short highlights. Tell us your usual video length and editing challenges in the application so we can prioritize useful workflows.

How is my application data used?

Your answers and email are stored for product research and early-access follow-up. The application does not collect footage or payment details.
