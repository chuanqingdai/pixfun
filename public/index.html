GOOD TO KNOW

The blueprint, explained.

What Pixfun studies, what you replace, and where originality begins.

What does Pixfun deconstruct?+

Pixfun’s product direction is to map the hook, shot order, scene length, pacing, transitions, caption rhythm, and music beats behind a reference edit.

Today’s local prototype supports import, scene detection, storyboard thumbnails, timeline segmentation, and timed text. Advanced transition and music analysis, asset replacement, translation, and dubbing remain product demos.

Does Pixfun copy the original video?+

No. The goal is to reuse creative strategy, not someone else’s footage or identity. A blueprint describes how the edit works; your final version should use your own authorized assets, story, and brand.

What can I replace in a blueprint?+

The intended workflow lets you replace footage, products, presenters, script, language, captions, brand elements, and music while preserving the useful structure and rhythm.

In the current prototype, you can save direction, split scenes, edit text, and export a vertical MP4. Automatic visual replacement is not connected yet.

Can I reuse the reference music?+

Only when you have the rights to use it on your destination platform. Otherwise, reuse the tempo and beat strategy with licensed music, platform-cleared audio, or a track you own.

Can I use a link or upload a file?+

Both. Upload a supported video file up to 500 MB, or import a supported video link. Link access depends on the platform, your connection, and your permissions.

What content am I allowed to analyze?+

Use content you own, are licensed to use, or have permission to analyze. Pixfun does not bypass private access, DRM, copyright, music rights, or platform rules.
